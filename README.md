A front-page newspaper collage summing up the world's news of
Tuesday 17 November 2026: US strikes on Iran's Qeshm Island, the
Kerch Bridge collapse, Trump's Quad summit in Manila, Nvidia's
earnings surge after Huang's exit, and the Houthi advance on Aden.

Entry for the [news-image-20261117 bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261117).

## The five stories

All five are real stories from Tuesday 17 November 2026:

1. US fighter jets strike an IRGC missile launch site on Iran's
   Qeshm Island; Iran drones a US radar in Bahrain.
2. Ukraine hits the Kerch Bridge again; a road section collapses
   into the strait.
3. Trump arrives in Manila for the Quad summit, with Xi and Putin
   talks ahead.
4. Nvidia reports bumper earnings after Huang's exit, as the US
   closes a chip-export loophole.
5. Houthi forces advance on Aden as Yemen's government troops
   retreat.

The picture is 1024x683 pixels and 250,777 bytes, inside the gate
limits (1 MiB, 1024x1024).

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the picture
shipped beside it (`entry.png`, JPEG bytes) to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). The program is
deterministic: every run writes the same bytes.

## Test

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
